PURPOSE

This document freezes the launch rules for $DUMP before the token is deployed.

The objective is to create a transparent, auditable meme-token experiment rather than manufacture a market outcome.

The phrase:

“THE PUMP AND THE DUMP.”

is part of the project’s creative origin and meme identity.

It is not an instruction, promise, or agreement to manipulate the token price.

⸻

DAY-0 RULES

1. NO PRESALE

There will be no private presale or preferential allocation before public deployment.

2. PUBLIC LAUNCH

The token will be created publicly through Pump.fun.

3. ONE DISCLOSED CREATOR WALLET

The primary creator wallet used for launch will be publicly disclosed after deployment.

4. INITIAL CREATOR PURCHASE

Maximum planned creator purchase at initial launch:

1 SOL

The actual amount and transaction signature will be recorded after execution.

5. NO GUARANTEED RETURN

No representation will be made that $DUMP will:

* increase in price
* reach a particular market capitalization
* generate income
* provide guaranteed returns
* maintain any particular value

6. NO COORDINATED PUMP

The creator will not organize coordinated buying intended to artificially increase the market price.

7. NO WASH TRADING

The creator will not use multiple controlled wallets to manufacture trading volume or the appearance of independent market demand.

8. NO HIDDEN CREATOR WALLET STRATEGY

Creator-controlled wallets used materially in the experiment should be disclosed in the public provenance record.

9. BOTS ARE OBSERVATIONS

If automated traders, snipers, or bots interact with the token, their activity will be treated as observational data.

The creator will not chase a bot transaction simply to restore a desired position on the curve.

10. MARKET OUTCOMES WILL BE RECORDED

Positive and negative outcomes may both be documented.

The experiment does not require the token to appreciate in price to be considered useful.

⸻

CREATOR CAPITAL POLICY

Total Day-0 capital available to the creator does not equal capital committed to $DUMP.

Initial creator exposure is intentionally limited.

Unused capital is not automatically designated for:

* supporting the chart
* buying dips
* defending market capitalization
* creating artificial volume
* counteracting sellers

Any later creator transaction should be independently documented.
